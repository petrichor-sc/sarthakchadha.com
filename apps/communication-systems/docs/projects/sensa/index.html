Research platform / SENSA

Making sensing
easier to begin.

SENSA explores a unified workflow for eye tracking and biosignals. My research focuses on the first steps: helping users understand setup, onboarding and calibration.

PracticeHuman–computer interactionFocusOnboarding & calibrationPublic materialResearch overview

Why the beginning matters

Collecting physiological and eye-tracking data involves a sequence of setup decisions before recording can begin. Device preparation and calibration are part of the user experience of a research tool.

SENSA brings this starting point into focus: how can a unified workflow help users understand what to do, what state the system is in and when they are ready to continue?

The intended workflow

01Set up

Understand devices and the requirements for a recording session.

02Calibrate

Follow the calibration process with clear feedback.

03Record

Move from preparation toward a recording workflow.

This sequence describes the research focus and intended experience.

My research focus

The project connects interface design and human–computer interaction with the practical demands of multimodal sensing. My thesis focus is onboarding and calibration.

This research story connects the practical needs of multimodal recording with the experience of the people setting it up.
