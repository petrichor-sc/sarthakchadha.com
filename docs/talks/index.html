Guest lecture / Hochschule Rhein-Waal

Scent, mind
& interaction.

Psychology for UX Practitioners

In a special lecture for the Psychology for UX Practitioners course, I introduced olfaction as an interaction modality, connecting the physiology of smell with multisensory user experience.

Students shared the memories evoked by a scent, observed a live visualization of biometric signals and developed scent-integrated UX concepts for fields including smart homes, sport, meditation and online perfumery.

The session brought theory, sensory exploration and design practice into the same room.

Public research talk / June 2025

Two pine forests.
One shared curiosity.

Science Café · Hochschule Rhein-Waal

At the Science Café, Raphael Komanek and I presented our research on ultrasonic-assisted extraction of aromatic botanicals.

Our collaboration began with a shared connection to pine: Raphael’s memories of Arolla pines in Tyrol and my own of Pinus roxburghii in Uttarakhand. His interest in taste and flavour met my work in perfumery and digital olfaction through botanical extraction.

We discussed our experimental work and closed with a smelling session, inviting participants to connect the research with their own scent memories.

Open lab / Public engagement

A more human
way into STEM.

Tag der offenen Tür · Usability Lab

For the university’s open day, we welcomed visitors into the Usability Lab to explore two projects: Synesthesia VR and Pepper the humanoid robot.

With Synesthesia, visitors experienced a digitally controlled olfactory display and explored scent as an interface. Pepper offered a friendly, hands-on introduction to human–robot interaction.

These demonstrations brought engineering and sensory experience together, inviting children and adults to discover the research through interaction.
